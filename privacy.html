Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and Squid Space never asks for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • The site itself doesn't use cookies, and has no analytics or tracking tools of its own.
  • The tools run in your browser. Unless a tool clearly says otherwise, what you type into a tool and any file you open in it stay on your device.
  • Squid Space shows ads from Google AdSense. Because children may use the site, we ask Google to treat it as made for children, which turns off personalized ads.
  • Squid Space shows ads from Google AdSense. These ads may be personalized, and you can control that in Google's Ads Settings (see “Ads” below).
  • Like every website, the site's host and the companies that deliver ads can see your internet address (IP address) when your browser connects.
  • Questions? Contact us at the email below.

Who we are

RJ's Squid Space (“Squid Space”, “we”, “us”) is a free website of software tools that run in your web browser. The site and its tools are run by one person, the site's owner. You can reach us using the contact details at the bottom of this page.

Kids and families

Squid Space is for everyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or from anyone else. The site has no accounts, no sign-ups, no comments and no chat.

Squid Space is made for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The site has no accounts, no sign-ups, no comments and no chat.

If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

What Squid Space collects and stores

Squid Space does not ask for, collect or store personal information. We do not sell or share personal information. We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

  • Cookies: the site itself sets none. (The ads do, see “Ads” below.)
  • Your files and work: tools run in your browser. Unless a tool clearly says otherwise, anything you type into a tool, and any file you open in it (such as a drawing or a 3D model), stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Downloads: when a tool lets you export or download a file, it is saved on your own device.
  • Saved work and settings: some tools save your work or settings in your browser's storage. This stays on your own device. You can clear it any time in your browser's settings, and doing so deletes that saved work.

Where the site is hosted

Squid Space is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). When your browser loads the site, GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you ask for, and may keep logs. We don't receive those logs. You can read how GitHub handles information in its privacy statement.

The “online now” counter

The box in the top left of the home page shows how many people are on Squid Space right now. To work it out, each open tab makes up a random code that is kept only in the tab's memory (not in cookies or storage), and sends that code and the time to a database run by Google (Firebase) every 45 seconds. The site counts the codes seen in the last two minutes. The code is deleted when you close or leave the tab, and any that are left behind disappear within a few minutes.

The code is random, contains nothing about you, and is not used for ads or to build a profile. As with any request over the internet, Google's servers can see your IP address when your browser contacts them.

Ads

Squid Space shows ads delivered by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a person's earlier visits to a website or to other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads to people based on their visits to sites across the internet.

Because children may use the site, we tell Google to treat every ad on Squid Space as child-directed. Google says that for content marked this way it takes steps to turn off interest-based advertising and remarketing ads. Ads can still appear, but they are not meant to follow you around based on sites you have visited. Google and its partners may still use cookies or similar technology to deliver and measure ads. We don't control those, and they are covered by Google's own policies.

You can control personalized advertising across the web at the links below. We don't control the cookies that Google and its partners set, and they are covered by Google's own policies.

You can manage personalized advertising at Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some other companies' use of cookies for personalized ads at aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its ad services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you may see a message asking about your choices for ads and cookies. Your answer is stored by the ad service, not by Squid Space.

How long information is kept

We do not keep personal information. The “online now” codes last only a few minutes. Information that GitHub, Google and ad companies receive is kept under their own policies.

Because we don't collect personal information, there is usually nothing to look up, but you are welcome to ask.
