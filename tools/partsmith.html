Partsmith

How Partsmith works

Draw a flat sketch, then turn it into a solid. Every step is saved in the feature tree on the left, so you can go back and change any number later and the part rebuilds.

Making a part

  1. Click a plane (Top, Front or Right) or a flat face of your part, then click Sketch.
  2. Draw rectangles, circles, polygons, slots or lines. Type numbers while drawing to set exact sizes.
  3. Use Smart Dimension (D) to lock sizes and positions. Click a line, circle or point, click a second item for a distance or angle, then click to place it and type the value. Dimensions keep driving the sketch, so a hole dimensioned from an edge follows that edge.
  4. Click Extrude to give the sketch thickness, Cut to remove material, or Revolve to spin it around an axis.
  5. Export STL and open it in your slicer.

Good to know

Shapes that overlap are merged. A shape inside another becomes a hole. Polygon sizes can be measured across corners or across flats, which suits hex nut pockets. The unit name at the bottom right switches between millimeters, centimeters, meters, inches, feet and yards. Everything is kept in millimeters underneath, so STL files always come out in millimeters, the size slicers expect. You can type a unit into any size, like 1.5in, 2ft or 5'6", and it converts. The mouse button next to the view bar switches between free rotation and turntable rotation, and can reverse the scroll zoom. Volume and solid weight in PLA are shown under the tree.
